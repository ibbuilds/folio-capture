# Next phase: aesthetics and selective curation

The first release collects an evidence-rich research inbox. It does not certify designs as excellent or assign automatic 9/10 scores. Our eventual published library should contain only work that clears our high editorial standard, judged within its intended aesthetic and purpose.

## Keep retrieval simple

The capture engine retains thirteen independent metadata axes so references can be retrieved precisely. The QA report exposes four default controls: aesthetic, composition, section purpose, and viewport. Search handles useful phrases; specialist metadata sits under More filters. The future Folio product should start with a similarly small set of validated user needs rather than expose every stored property.

## Research before expanding the taxonomy

The current sixteen aesthetic labels are a provisional controlled vocabulary. They are not an exhaustive history of design or a list of fashionable treatments. Mixed labels and unknowns are valid. Color, type scale, layout, imagery, and surface treatment stay distinct, so a dark page is not assigned an aesthetic merely because it is dark.

The research phase should assemble contemporary, clearly sourced examples for each useful aesthetic family, identify observable distinguishing traits and confusing neighbors, and remove synonyms or labels that do not improve retrieval. Each accepted definition needs positive examples, counterexamples, evidence requirements, and reviewer agreement. A year, award, brand, or trend label does not establish execution quality.

Initial reading leads from the preliminary source search (not a completed research review):

- [NN/g: Neobrutalism](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/neobrutalism/) — distinguishing raw visual traits from conventional minimalism.
- [NN/g: Aesthetic and Minimalist Design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/aesthetic-minimalist-design/) — relevance and information priorities do not mandate a minimalist visual style.
- [NN/g: Flat-Design Best Practices](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/flat-design-best-practices/) — preserving signifiers and usable hierarchy within flat treatments.
- [Fluent 2: Material](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/material) — named material treatments belong to their visible role; a material alone does not establish an entire aesthetic.
- [Material 3 foundations](https://m3.material.io/foundations/) — foundation, style, and component decisions can be classified independently.
- [Awwwards evaluation](https://www.awwwards.com/about-evaluation/) — a research lead for an external editorial rubric; its scores must not be substituted for our internal acceptance standard.

## Calibrate the 9–10 standard

Build a human-reviewed benchmark with excellent, adequate, weak, and borderline examples within each aesthetic and page purpose. Include all viewport families and distinguish source-design flaws from capture defects. Compare candidates against anchored examples for composition, hierarchy, typography, readability, visual consistency, responsive execution, and fulfillment of the intended experience. Style fidelity is one dimension, not a substitute for these fundamentals.

Use independent reviews and explain disagreements. A future model may propose evidence-based judgments, but its confidence and rubric version must be recorded, and uncertain or unsupported results stay pending. Validate false accepts, false rejects, reviewer agreement, and calibration on held-out examples before setting an automated threshold. An uncalibrated score of 9 is a number, not proof of excellence.

Promotion should be explicit: collected → review pending → accepted or rejected. Capture completeness, aesthetic classification, and editorial acceptance are separate decisions. Preserve rejected research samples for calibration without putting them into the curated library. Missing media, obstruction, unreadable crops, and unsupported aesthetic claims should prevent automatic promotion. Improvements to capture or taxonomy invalidate affected reviews rather than silently reuse approval.

The foundation deliberately leaves this gate unimplemented until the benchmark, rubric, and intended retrieval experience have been researched with us.
