# Aesthetics and selective curation

The completed [research review](classification-research.md), [useful source inventory](reference-sources.md), and [implementation plan](classification-plan.md) replace the preliminary reading leads previously kept here.

Our standard is excellent, usable design within its intended aesthetic. Publication date does not determine quality. Recent curated collections and award selections help discover contemporary work; excellent older references remain eligible.

The plan defines a compact aesthetic vocabulary, evidence requirements and counterexamples, a hybrid classifier using existing Codex access, human calibration, and a separately governed 9–10 curated export. Clean, minimal, glassmorphism, neumorphism, brutalism, and neobrutalism remain understandable and searchable without creating a large collection of filters.

Quality review focuses on hierarchy, typography/readability, consistency, aesthetic coherence, useful content, and responsive execution. Basic accessibility guidance serves those fundamentals; exhaustive compliance auditing is outside the acceptance requirement.

The current engine collects and provisionally classifies references. Its contract tests and captured samples do not validate elite editorial judgment. The benchmark, taxonomy v2, durable review workflow, extra adapters, and quality gate are planned work. Automatically captured references remain unapproved.
