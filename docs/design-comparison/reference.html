A handoff works when the next team can see what matters and what to do with it.

  1. 01

    Bring the conversation.

    Start where the work took shape. Keep the questions, notes, and decisions together.

    What you bringThe discussion and its context
  2. 02

    Shape the handoff.

    Separate what was decided from why it matters and what still needs an answer.

    What you clarifyDecision · reasoning · open questions
  3. 03

    Give the next team a start.

    Make the owner and the next action explicit. Keep the original context within reach.

    What you pass forwardAn owner and a concrete next step
